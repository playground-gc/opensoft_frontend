Performance Benchmarks

Numbers that
don't lie.

Six hard constraints that keep the terminal running at full speed in real market conditions — not just in demos.

60fps
CHART REFRESH RATE
Candles batch and render once per screen frame. Completely smooth at any tick rate — your charts never fall behind the market.
O(1)
ORDER BOOK SPEED
Each bid or ask update is a single constant-time operation. The book stays instant no matter how fast prices cascade across levels.
1ws
LIVE CONNECTION
One connection handles everything. Price, book, portfolio, bots — all flowing through a single persistent stream that cannot desync.
10fps
P&L UPDATE RATE
Your profit and loss refresh 10 times per second. Fast enough to trade on. Stable enough to actually read without the numbers blurring.
50rows
BOT FEED CAP
Live feed shows 50 rows, capped at 200 in memory. Fast and fluid even at 100 automated trades per second — your terminal never crashes.
0lag
CHART OVERHEAD
Chart updates go straight to the GPU graphics layer. Zero overhead per candle tick — no UI framework cycles burning between you and the market.
